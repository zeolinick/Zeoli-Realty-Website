# Zeoli Realty — Brand Guidelines

*Vol. 01 · Relaunch 2026 · Southeast Michigan*

Zeoli Realty is a premier group of real estate professionals serving Southeast Michigan. Founded by Nick Zeoli in 2021, the team has closed $150M+ in sales. The brand is **warm, established, and confident** — a boutique alternative to the big portals.

---

## 1. Logo

**The mark is a doorway arch** — the threshold of a home, the welcome in. It is architectural and warm. It pairs with a clean sans wordmark.

**Variants**
- **Primary / Horizontal** — mark + "Zeoli Realty" side by side.
- **Stacked** — mark above "Zeoli" with "REALTY" tracked beneath.
- **Mark only** — for avatars, favicons, stamps. Use down to 24px digital / 0.25in print.
- **Reversed** — cream mark + cream wordmark on Evergreen or Ink backgrounds.

**Rules**
- Clear space = half the mark's height on all sides.
- The wordmark is always **Plus Jakarta Sans** (sans), title case: "Zeoli" 700 ink, "Realty" 600 stone.
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate/tilt, recolor off-brand, italicize the wordmark, or place on busy imagery.

## 2. Color — Black & White

*The team paused the green/clay palette; the brand runs monochrome for now (revisit color later).*

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| **Ink** | `#141414` | 20 · 20 · 20 | Primary brand, headlines, buttons, the mark, dark fields |
| **Paper** | `#FFFFFF` | 255 · 255 · 255 | Primary background |
| **Bone** | `#F4F2EC` | 244 · 242 · 236 | Warm surfaces / cards / reversed text |
| **Stone** | `#8C8B7E` | 140 · 139 · 126 | The "Realty" in the wordmark, quiet labels |
| **Graphite** | `#6B6B66` | 107 · 107 · 102 | Mid-tone fields, secondary buttons |
| **Slate** | `#7C7A72` | 124 · 122 · 114 | Secondary body text |

**Ratio:** white & bone lead · Ink carries the brand · grays handle hierarchy. No accent hue — contrast and the serif do the work.

## 3. Typography

- **Display — Newsreader** (warm editorial serif). Headlines, big numbers, pull-quotes. Weights 500–600, with *italic* for accent words ("…feel like *home.*"). This serif is the brand's signature — it's what sets us apart from sans-only portals, and it carries the personality now that color is monochrome.
- **Text / UI / Wordmark — Plus Jakarta Sans.** Body copy, labels, buttons, interface, and the logo wordmark. Weights 400–800. Eyebrows: 700, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 0.14em.

Google Fonts:
- `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Newsreader:ital,opsz,wght@0,6..72,400;0,6..72,500;0,6..72,600;1,6..72,500;1,6..72,600&display=swap`
- `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap`

## 4. Signature Device — the Arch

Crop property and lifestyle photography with an **arched top** (rounded top corners → semicircle for square images). It echoes the doorway mark and makes our materials recognizable on sight.

## 5. Voice

- **Confident, not loud** — state results plainly; let the work speak.
- **Warm & clear** — warm with people, exact with details.
- **Built for the long term** — talk about futures and partnership, not just transactions.

**We say:** "Your goals are our mission." · "Expert guidance. A seamless experience." · "Partners in building futures."
**We avoid:** hype & exclamation overload, jargon, generic "your dream home" filler.
